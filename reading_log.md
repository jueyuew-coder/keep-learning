
### REACT : SYNERGIZING REASONING AND ACTING IN LANGUAGE MODEL
记录日期：2026-10-25
文献年份：2022
**研究问题**：LLMs’ abilities for reasoning and acting have primarily been studied as separate topics  Want to allow for greater synergy between reasoning and acting
**研究方法**：标准提示方法，思维链提示方法，仅行动提示方法，将ReAct 和CoT-SC 结合等
**结论**：ReAct is an efficient and simple method of synergizing reasoning and action and leads to superior performance.Despite its simplicity,complex tasks with large action sapces require more demonstrations.
